You are given few useful crafting recipes to craft items in Minecraft. The actions you can perform are of the following format:
1. When you want to craft something, use: "craft object using ingredients". 
2. When you want to check your inventory, use: "inventory".
3. When you want to get ingredients, use: "get ingredients".
4. When you do not know the specific action to take next, make a plan, start with "think: ".
Note that only when you are difinitely sure about what to do next, for example, "Next, I will do: command", can you take actions "craft","inventory" or "get", or you should "think".

Here is an example.
Crafting commands:
craft 3 dark oak sign using 6 dark oak planks, 1 stick
craft 4 dark oak planks using 1 dark oak log
craft 1 stick using 2 bamboo
craft 4 oak planks using 1 oak log
craft 1 dark oak fence using 2 stick, 4 dark oak planks
craft 1 warped stairs using 6 warped planks
craft 3 oak sign using 6 oak planks, 1 stick

Goal: craft dark oak sign

> think: To get target objects, since the command is "craft 3 dark oak sign using 6 dark oak planks, 1 stick", I need to get 6 dark oak planks. Since dark oak planks is craftable and the command is "craft 4 dark oak planks using 1 dark oak log", I need to continue to check recipes.
OK.
> think: To get target objects, since the command is "craft 4 dark oak planks using 1 dark oak log", I need to get 2 dark oak log. Since dark oak log is not craftable, next I will do: "get 2 dark oak log".
OK.
> get 2 dark oak log
Got 2 dark oak log
> think: To craft target objects, since the command is "craft 4 dark oak planks using 1 dark oak log" and I got all materials, next I will do: "craft 4 dark oak planks using 1 dark oak log" for 2 times.

> craft 4 dark oak planks using 1 dark oak log
